About

A studio built around accountability.

Nexora Studio designs and builds mobile applications, high-performance web platforms and applied AI systems — for companies and for products of our own.

We started by building our own products: Arise, an AI guide to university admissions, and DigitalLib, a digital library for Uzbek readers. Both are live and we still run them. Now we build for clients too.

What we believe

Four rules we don't break.

  • You talk to the team.

    You work directly with the designers and engineers on your project. No layers of managers between you and the work.

  • Small by design.

    We take on a few projects at a time, so each one gets real attention.

  • Honest about limits.

    If something can't be done well, or we are not the right team, we say so on the first call — not after the invoice.

  • We stay after launch.

    Releases, monitoring and iteration are part of the job. We run our own products the same way.

Process

From first call to production.

Four clear stages, with something you can see and test at each one.

  1. Week 0

    Scope

    An intro call, a written brief and a clear plan.

  2. Week 1–2

    Architect

    Data model, interfaces and the risky parts proven first.

  3. Week 3+

    Build

    Weekly builds you can install, with a live changelog.

  4. Ongoing

    Operate

    Releases, monitoring and iteration after launch day.

Questions

Before you write to us.

Who will actually work on my product?

Our own team: the same designers and engineers you meet on the first call. We do not outsource the work.

How quickly can we start?

Write to us with what you are building. We reply within one working day and agree the start date together. We take on a few projects at a time, so each one gets proper attention.

What does a typical project look like?

Week 0 is an intro call and a written brief with a clear plan. Weeks 1–2 prove the architecture and the risky parts. From week 3 you get weekly builds you can install, with a list of changes — and after launch we keep supporting it.

Which technologies do you use?

React Native, Expo and Swift for mobile; React, Vite and Supabase on the web; LLMs, retrieval pipelines and Python for applied AI; Three.js and WebGL for interactive 3D. We pick what your product needs, not what is fashionable.

Do you work with clients outside Uzbekistan?

Yes. We are based in Fergana (UTC+5) and ship worldwide. We work in English, Russian and Uzbek.
