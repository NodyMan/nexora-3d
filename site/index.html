About

A studio built around accountability.

Nexora Studio designs and builds mobile applications, high-performance web platforms and applied AI systems — for companies and for products of our own.

Our benchmark is not the local market. It is the finest digital work produced anywhere. That standard shows up in architecture, performance and the small details users feel before they can name them.

More about the studio

History

Two products first. Then a studio.

Nexora did not begin as an agency. It began with products the founders had to design, ship and run themselves.

  1. Origin

    It started close to home.

    Students in Uzbekistan finding their way to university on their own. Readers piecing books together from scattered Telegram channels. We decided to build the answers ourselves.

  2. Then

    We learned by shipping.

    Before taking a single client, the three of us designed, built and ran products of our own — an AI mentor for future students and a national reading library. Every decision was tested on real users, not in a pitch deck.

  3. 2026

    The collaboration became a studio.

    Nexora Studio was founded in Fergana to bring the same standard — strategy, design, engineering and operations under one roof — to other teams.

  4. Today

    Two tracks, one team.

    We build for clients and keep running our own products. The same three founders scope, build and support everything — nothing is handed to people you have never met.

What we believe

Four rules we don't break.

  • No intermediaries.

    The founder on your first call is the person who architects, builds and answers for your product. No account managers, no relay race.

  • Small by design.

    We keep only a few projects running at once, so every one gets the full attention of the people who started the studio.

  • Honest about limits.

    If something can't be done well, or we are not the right team, we say so on the first call — not after the invoice.

  • We stay after launch.

    Releases, monitoring and iteration are part of the job. We run our own products the same way.

Process

From first call to production.

Four stages, no relay race. The founders stay on the project from the first sketch to the release and beyond.

  1. Week 0

    Scope

    A founder call, a written brief and a fixed plan. No sales layer.

  2. Week 1–2

    Architect

    Data model, interfaces and the risky parts proven first.

  3. Week 3+

    Build

    Weekly builds you can install, with a live changelog.

  4. Ongoing

    Operate

    Releases, monitoring and iteration after launch day.

Questions

Before you write to us.

Who will actually work on my product?

The three founders. The people on your scoping call are the people writing the code, running the releases and answering your messages — there is no account manager or outsourced layer in between.

How quickly can we start?

Write to us with what you are building. A founder replies personally, and we agree the start date together — we keep only a few projects running at once, so each one gets full attention.

What does a typical project look like?

Week 0 is a founder call and a written brief with a fixed plan. Weeks 1–2 prove the architecture and the risky parts. From week 3 you get weekly builds you can install, with a live changelog — and after launch we keep operating it.

Which technologies do you use?

React Native, Expo and Swift for mobile; React, Vite and Supabase on the web; LLMs, retrieval pipelines and Python for applied AI; Three.js and WebGL for interactive 3D. We pick what your product needs, not what is fashionable.

Do you work with clients outside Uzbekistan?

Yes. We are based in Fergana (UTC+5) and ship worldwide. We work in English, Russian and Uzbek.
