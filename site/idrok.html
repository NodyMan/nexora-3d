01 — The problem

Books were scattered across Telegram channels.

Readers in Uzbekistan looked for books in dozens of Telegram channels and on old websites that were never made for a phone. Files were hard to find, hard to read and easy to lose.

We set out to put them in one place that is pleasant to read in and light enough for any phone.

02 — What we built

One library, a good reader and an AI librarian.

01

My Library

Continue reading where you stopped, keep your own books and PDFs, and browse the collection.

02

Comfortable reader

Three display modes, day, sepia, dark and night themes, adjustable fonts, text-to-speech with speed control, bookmarks and notes.

03

Alif, the AI librarian

Alif finds pages, quotes books from the library, explains key ideas and makes a personal reading plan.

04

Arena

Quizzes on book chapters with XP and reader ranks, so reading turns into a habit.

03 — Engineering

Light, not stripped down.

The Android app weighs 7.05 MB. That is not a limit we set for every product: it is what this one needed, because many readers use older phones and limited mobile data.

  • Chapters load as you read instead of downloading the whole catalogue
  • Only the pages you have opened are saved on the phone
  • Covers are prepared in light image formats for every screen size
  • No third-party SDK slows down the start of the app

The result is an app a student can download on mobile data without thinking about it, with nothing cut to get there. Each product gets the size its job needs; what carries over is the habit of making every kilobyte earn its place.
