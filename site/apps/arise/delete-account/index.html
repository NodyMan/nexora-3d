Delete your Arise account

Last updated: 25 November 2026

You can delete your Arise account and all your data at any time. Deletion is permanent and can’t be undone.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Arise and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the warning and tap Yes, delete forever.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

Send an email to support@nexorastudio.world with the subject “Delete my Arise account”. Write from the email address you use in Arise, or tell us how you sign in (Google or Apple). We will confirm and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your profile, interests, chosen direction and universities
  • Your portfolio and motivation letter
  • AI analysis, skill plan, letter reviews, project ideas and Ari chat history
  • XP, skill progress, streaks and leaderboard entries

Data is removed from our live database immediately; backup copies are erased within 30 days. We don’t keep any of your data after that, unless the law requires it.

Only want to start over?

In Profile you can also tap Start setup again to redo the questionnaire without deleting your account.
