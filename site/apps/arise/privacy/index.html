Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the Arise mobile app (“Arise”, “we”, “us”) collects, why we collect it, who we share it with and what choices you have. Arise is made by Nexora Studio, operated by Makhmud Nasriddinov, an independent developer based in Tashkent, Uzbekistan.

Short version: we collect only what Arise needs to give you study advice, we don’t sell your data, we don’t show ads, and you can delete your account and data at any time inside the app.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Nexora Studio, operated by Makhmud Nasriddinov, Tashkent, Uzbekistan. Contact: support@nexorastudio.world.

2. What we collect

Account data. Depending on how you sign in:

  • Email sign-up: your name, email address and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Google sign-in: your name, email address and Google account ID.
  • Sign in with Apple: your name (if you share it), email address or Apple private relay address, and Apple account ID.
  • Guest mode: an anonymous ID only.

Age and consent. Your year of birth, whether a parent or guardian agreed (for users aged 13–15), and the date you accepted these terms.

Your answers in the app. Interests and the text you write about yourself, the direction and career you choose, saved and dream universities, and your portfolio (grades, test scores, certificates, projects and motivation letter).

Results created for you. Your AI analysis, skill plan, letter review, project ideas and your chat with Ari.

Progress. XP, completed skills and streaks.

Technical data. Counters of AI requests (to apply daily limits) and standard server logs kept by our hosting providers, which can include IP address, device and app version.

Photos and voice (iPhone). If you use “Scan document” or send a voice message to Ari, the photo or recording is sent to Google Gemini only to read it. Arise does not store the photo or the recording; it saves only the text you confirm and Ari’s reply.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photo library or advertising IDs. Reminders and calendar events are created only on your device.

3. How we use it

  • To run Arise: create your account, save your answers and show your plan and progress.
  • To generate personal recommendations with AI (see section 4).
  • To show the weekly leaderboard: only your first name, the first letter of your last name and your XP are visible to other users.
  • To keep Arise safe: prevent abuse and apply fair-use limits.
  • To answer your support requests.

We process your data because you asked us to provide the service (contract) and because you consented when you started using Arise. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. AI processing

Arise uses the Google Gemini API to create recommendations. When you use an AI feature, the relevant part of your profile (for example interests, chosen direction, portfolio or motivation letter) and your messages to Ari are sent to Google for processing. We use Gemini under Google’s paid API terms, under which Google does not use this content to train its models.

Please don’t share sensitive information (such as health data, passport numbers or passwords) in free text or in the chat.

AI results are estimates. They may be wrong and are not a guarantee of admission or of any salary.

5. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that help us run Arise, and only as needed:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and server functions.
  • Google — Gemini AI processing (including documents you scan and voice messages to Ari); Google Sign-In if you choose it.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, if you choose it.
  • Vercel — hosting of this website.
  • Cloudflare — domain name service and our support email.

We may disclose data if the law requires it. If Nexora Studio is ever reorganised or sold, this policy will continue to protect your data, and we will tell you first.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in data centres of our provider Supabase, which may be located outside Uzbekistan. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and database access rules let each user read only their own data.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our live database immediately. Copies in backups are erased within 30 days. AI request counters are kept only as long as needed to apply limits.

8. Your rights and choices

  • See and change your answers and portfolio in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account and all data: Profile → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Get a copy of your data or ask any question by writing to support@nexorastudio.world.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.
  • Complain to the data protection authority in your country.

We answer requests within 30 days.

9. Children

Arise is for people aged 13 and older. Users aged 13 to 15 may use Arise only with the agreement of a parent or guardian, which we ask for when the account is set up. If we learn that a user is under 13, we delete the account and its data. Parents and guardians can contact us at support@nexorastudio.world to review or delete their child’s data.

10. Security

We use encrypted connections, row-level security in the database and keep secret keys only on the server, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If the changes are important, we will tell you in the app before they apply.
