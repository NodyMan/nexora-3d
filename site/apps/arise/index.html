Questions

Good to know.

Who is Arise for?

School students who are choosing a profession and a university. Arise is for people aged 13 and older; if you are 13 to 15, a parent or guardian needs to agree when you sign up.

How much does it cost?

Arise is free to start. If we add paid features later, the price will be shown clearly in the app before you pay.

When can I download it?

Arise is in testing now and is coming to Google Play soon. Write to us if you want to try it early.

Which languages does the app speak?

O‘zbekcha, Русский and English. Ari answers in the language you write in.

Are the admission chances a guarantee?

No. Match scores, chances and salaries are estimates. Always check requirements and deadlines on the university’s official website.

What happens to my data?

We collect only what Arise needs to help you, we never sell it and there are no ads. You can delete your account and all your data at any time.
