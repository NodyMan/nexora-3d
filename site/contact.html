03 — Questions we often get
How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After the first call we send a written estimate with a fixed scope and timeline, so you know the cost before work starts.

How long does it take?

Most first versions take from several weeks to a few months, depending on the features. You see working builds every week, so there are no surprises at the end.

Who will actually work on my product?

Our own team: the same designers and engineers you meet on the first call. We do not outsource the work.

Can you take over an existing product?

Yes. We start with a short review of the code and design, then tell you honestly what to keep, what to fix and what to rebuild.

Do you work with clients outside Uzbekistan?

Yes. We are based in Fergana (UTC+5) and ship worldwide. We work in English, Russian and Uzbek.
